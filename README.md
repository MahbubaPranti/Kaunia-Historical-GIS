# Historical GIS Cartography: River Migration & Transport Network Changes in Kaunia, Rangpur (1927–2026)

## Project Overview
This project investigates 99 years of environmental and infrastructural transformations in Kaunia Upazila, Rangpur District, Bangladesh (1927–2026). Using QGIS, this study compares colonial-era cartographic records with contemporary spatial datasets to track long-term shifts in river channels, road networks, and railway lines.

## Methodology & Data Sources
A 1927 colonial quarter-inch topographic map (Assam, Bengal, Bhutan, Eastern States) was georeferenced and manually digitized in QGIS to extract historical rivers, roads, and railway tracks. These historical vector layers were then overlaid and compared against modern 2026 OpenStreetMap (OSM) datasets and administrative boundaries to analyze spatial changes over time.

## Conference Presentation
This cartographic research was accepted for presentation at the International Online Workshop "Beyond Borders: Global Histories of Transboundary Rivers", organized by the Department of History & Centre for Economic and Social History, University of Ostrava, Czech Republic (November 19–20, 2026).

## Cartographic Outputs Included
- Study Area Map (study area.png): Administrative location of Kaunia Upazila within Rangpur District.
- River Network Changes (River.png): Centennial shift and migration of river channels (1927–2026).
- Road Network Changes (Roads.png): Historical versus modern road network expansion (1927–2026).
- Rail Network Changes (Railway.png): Continuity and transformation of railway alignments (1927–2026).

## Tools Used
QGIS, OpenStreetMap (OSM), Historical Topographic Survey Maps.
